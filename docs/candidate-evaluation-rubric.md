# Candidate quality review template

This is a proposed instrument to prepare human evaluation. It is not a product score, it has no criterion weights, and it must not be used for automated ranking until reviewers agree that the anchors fit the target languages and content types.

## Review scale

Score each dimension from 1 to 5. Use 1 for poor or unusable, 3 for acceptable with a clear correction, and 5 for strong with no material correction needed. Use 2 or 4 when the result falls between those anchors. Record a short note for scores of 1 or 2.

| Dimension | Review question |
| --- | --- |
| Completeness | Does the clip preserve enough setup and payoff to make the thought understandable? |
| Clarity | Can a viewer follow the spoken idea without extra context? |
| Relevance | Does the moment fit the creator's topic and intended audience? |
| Emphasis or novelty | Does the moment contain a useful insight, contrast, surprise, or memorable emphasis? |
| Audio quality | Is the speech intelligible and free of distracting audio problems? |
| Editability | Can the boundaries be adjusted into a usable short without cutting words or losing meaning? |

Do not total or average these scores yet. Do not treat them as engagement or virality predictions. Reviewers should score independently before discussing disagreements.

## Sample plan

Before evaluating, select at least three permission-cleared recordings covering the intended languages and audio conditions. Keep the recordings, reference transcripts, and completed sheets outside Git. Include candidates from the full source timeline, not only manually selected examples. Record transcript model/revision and source duration so errors can be traced to transcription or selection.

The blank row format is in [`candidate-evaluation-template.csv`](candidate-evaluation-template.csv). A human review and agreed target sample set remain required before the product changes candidate order or presents a quality score.
